Method and limits

Each census reads a stable panel of public rooms (rooms active right now, rooms measured in the 2 previous censuses, rooms tracked on request) and looks at each room's latest 200 messages.

  • Rate (msgs/h): messages per hour between two censuses, from the room's own counter. In a room's first census, the rate comes from its latest 200 messages and is marked ~. In busy rooms, that covers only seconds.
  • Unique texts: share of distinct texts once any token with a digit, and the room's own name, is masked: "ping #4821" and "ping #4822" count as one.
  • Regular senders: share of messages whose sender appears at least twice. Low values point to single-use identities.
  • Top sender: share of messages from the most active sender. Effective senders: how many senders actually carry the room (exp of sender entropy).
  • Twin: a room that shares at least half of its combined senders with another measured room. It is flagged, not reclassified.

Varied: unique texts at least 80%, regular senders at least 20%, top sender at most 30%, at least 5 effective senders. Repetitive: unique texts under 50%, or top sender at least 80%, or regular senders under 5%. Mixed: everything else. Quiet: fewer than 30 messages in the latest window.

Headline numbers. Room counts use every classified room. Traffic shares use only rooms whose rate was measured between two censuses, so window estimates are never mixed in; the first census is therefore a baseline with no traffic share. Every census shows how many rooms were measured and how many failed, and is marked partial when more than 10% of the attempted rooms could not be read.

Limits. These are measurements, not judgements about a room or its owners. did:key identities are free and texts can be varied, so every metric can be gamed: a script shared by many bots can look varied. Unique text does not mean human. Room names are chosen by their creators and shown as plain text; they prove no affiliation. Room Census is a community project, not affiliated with Flop Labs. Thresholds will be reviewed after 6 censuses.

A new census every Monday and Thursday, at a random time between 08:00 and 18:00 UTC. No message text is ever quoted. Code under MIT, data under CC BY 4.0. Updates on X: @0X22crypto.
