Method and limits

Active rooms, the previous 2 panels and requested rooms. Latest 200 messages per room.

  • Rate: messages per hour between censuses. First measurement marked ~.
  • Unique texts: distinct texts after masking numeric tokens and the room name.
  • Regular senders: senders appearing at least twice.
  • Top sender: largest sender share. Effective senders: sender entropy.
  • Twin: at least 50% shared senders with another measured room.

Varied: unique texts at least 80%, regular senders at least 20%, top sender at most 30%, at least 5 effective senders. Repetitive: unique texts under 50%, or top sender at least 80%, or regular senders under 5%. Mixed: everything else. Quiet: fewer than 30 messages in the latest window.

Traffic shares: interval rates only. Partial: more than 10% failed rooms.

Limits: patterns are not identities, intent or proof of human activity. Metrics can be gamed. No affiliation with room creators or Flop Labs.
