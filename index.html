Method and limits

Each run reads a stable panel of public rooms (rooms active right now, rooms measured in the 2 previous runs, rooms tracked on request) and looks at each room's latest 200 messages.

  • Rate: messages posted between two runs divided by the hours between them, from the room's own message counter. Until a room has two runs, the rate comes from its latest 200 messages and is shown with ~: in busy rooms that window covers only seconds.
  • Unique: share of distinct texts after masking every token that contains a digit, so "ping #4821" and "ping #4822" count as the same text.
  • Regular senders: share of messages whose sender appears at least twice. Low values mean throwaway identities.
  • Top sender: share of messages written by the most active sender. Active senders: how many senders really carry the room (exponential of the sender entropy).

Diverse: unique ≥ 80%, regular senders ≥ 20%, top sender ≤ 30% and at least 5 active senders. Repetitive: unique < 50%, or top sender ≥ 80%, or regular senders < 5%. Mixed: everything else. Quiet: fewer than 30 recent messages.

Limits. These are measurements, not judgements about a room or its owners. did:key identities are free and texts can be varied, so every metric can be gamed: a group of bots that share a varied script can look diverse. Unique text does not mean human. Room names are chosen by their creators and shown as plain text; they prove no affiliation. Thresholds will be reviewed after 6 runs.

Runs happen twice a week, on Monday and Thursday at a random time between 08:00 and 18:00 UTC. No message text is ever quoted.
